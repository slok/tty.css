Components

Cards

A semantic article is the card primitive. Its direct title, header, and footer children select the visual structure without changing the document meaning.

Direct article titles

A heading directly inside an article stays within the card body. Direct h1 and h2 titles share the larger article-title style; direct h3 through h6 share the smaller style. Choose the level from the page outline, not its appearance.

Deployment summary

A level-two title introduces this standalone article.

Replica health

A lower-level title introduces a card within the current section.

<div class="grid">
  <article>
    <h2>Deployment summary</h2>
    <p>A level-two title introduces this standalone article.</p>
  </article>
  <article>
    <h3>Replica health</h3>
    <p>A lower-level title introduces a card within the current section.</p>
  </article>
</div>

Header band

A direct header creates a full-width muted band. It may contain a heading and other genuinely introductory content; direct headings inside it receive a consistent card-title size.

Production cluster

eu-west · healthy

All replicas are serving traffic within the latency objective.

<article>
  <header>
    <h3>Production cluster</h3>
    <small>eu-west · healthy</small>
  </header>
  <p>All replicas are serving traffic within the latency objective.</p>
</article>

Border title

Add .legend to a direct article header to place that header over the top border. This is an article-header treatment, not the native legend element; use fieldset and legend for form groups.

Cost calculator

Estimate monthly storage and query volume before provisioning.

<article>
  <header class="legend">
    <h3>Cost calculator</h3>
  </header>
  <p>Estimate monthly storage and query volume before provisioning.</p>
</article>

Footer

A direct footer creates a separated muted band for metadata, related links, or actions. Keep destination links as links and actions as buttons.

Nightly backup

The latest snapshot completed successfully.

Updated 2 minutes agoView backup log
<article>
  <h3>Nightly backup</h3>
  <p>The latest snapshot completed successfully.</p>
  <footer>
    <div class="group">
      <small>Updated 2 minutes ago</small>
      <a href="#footer">View backup log</a>
      <button type="button" class="secondary">Run backup</button>
    </div>
  </footer>
</article>

Complete structure

Header and footer bands compose around the article body. Use ordinary headings within the body when it contains its own sections.

Search service

Version 4.8.1

The service is available in three regions.

Current status

All checks are passing.

Open service details
<article>
  <header>
    <h3>Search service</h3>
    <small>Version 4.8.1</small>
  </header>
  <p>The service is available in three regions.</p>
  <h4>Current status</h4>
  <p>All checks are passing.</p>
  <footer><a href="#complete">Open service details</a></footer>
</article>
