    Tooltips

    Supplementary visual hints with placement options and accessible-description guidance.

    Usage

    data-tooltip supplies the tooltip text. Add data-placement="bottom", left, or right to change its position; top is the default. Tooltips appear on hover and keyboard focus, and left and right placements fall back above the trigger on narrow screens.

    <p class="group">
  <button type="button" data-tooltip="Runs the current deployment">Top</button>
  <button type="button" class="secondary" data-tooltip="Shown below the control" data-placement="bottom">Bottom</button>
  <button type="button" class="outline" data-tooltip="Shown to the left" data-placement="left">Left</button>
  <button type="button" class="contrast" data-tooltip="Shown to the right" data-placement="right">Right</button>
</p>

    Tooltip overlays are shadowless and do not support frame treatments.

    The visual bubble is CSS generated content. It has no tooltip role, cannot be dismissed independently, is unavailable to touch-only interaction, and is not a reliable accessible description. Never put essential information in it. Keep the control's accessible name complete, and connect necessary help text with aria-describedby.

    Starts deployment immediately and cannot be paused.
    <button type="button" aria-describedby="deploy-help" data-tooltip="Starts deployment">Deploy</button>
<span class="sr-only" id="deploy-help">Starts deployment immediately and cannot be paused.</span>
