    Pagination

    Numbered pages, cursor-based links, and sequential content navigation.

    Pagination

    Add .pagination to a labelled nav for links that share the framework’s secondary button styling, including borders, offset shadows, hover, and pressed states, and wrap within their container. No .button class is needed on individual links. Use direct links or a direct ul or ol; numbered pages are optional. Do not add .menu or button roles. URLs, cursor values, page counts, and available destinations belong to your application; tty.css supplies styling only.

    Numbered pages

    Use aria-current="page" on the current page link or a non-interactive span. The current page uses the primary button fill and pressed appearance, with an underline that identifies it without color. Unavailable spans share the disabled button treatment; ellipses and result summaries stay plain text. Give numeric links descriptive accessible names.

    • Previous
    • 1
    • 2
    • 3
    • …
    • 20
    • Next
    Showing 21–40 of 400
    <nav class="pagination" aria-label="Search result pages">
  <ul>
    <li><a href="?page=1" rel="prev">Previous</a></li>
    <li><a href="?page=1" aria-label="Page 1">1</a></li>
    <li><a href="?page=2" aria-label="Page 2" aria-current="page">2</a></li>
    <li><a href="?page=3" aria-label="Page 3">3</a></li>
    <li><span aria-label="More pages">…</span></li>
    <li><a href="?page=20" aria-label="Page 20">20</a></li>
    <li><a href="?page=3" rel="next">Next</a></li>
  </ul>
  <small>Showing 21–40 of 400</small>
</nav>

    Cursor-based pagination

    Previous and next links work without page numbers, a current-page marker, or a known total. Use the opaque cursors returned by your application in real URLs. The optional result summary is ordinary text, not an automatic live announcement.

    PreviousNextShowing 20 events
    <nav class="pagination" aria-label="Event batches">
  <a href="?before=evt_021" rel="prev">Previous</a>
  <a href="?after=evt_040" rel="next">Next</a>
  <small>Showing 20 events</small>
</nav>

    Boundaries and sequential content

    Omit an unavailable destination, or replace its link with <span aria-disabled="true">. A span has no link behavior and is not in the Tab order. Do not use disabled on links, fake URLs, or aria-disabled alone on a working link. Sequential content can use descriptive labels instead of page numbers.

    Newer postsOlder posts →
    <nav class="pagination" aria-label="Post archives">
  <span aria-disabled="true">Newer posts</span>
  <a href="?before=post_040" rel="next">Older posts →</a>
</nav>

    These previews demonstrate markup only; their query strings do not change the sample results. Real links retain normal Tab and Enter behavior, bookmarking, and opening in another tab. Icon-only links need an accessible name. If your application replaces results without navigation, it also owns focus management and result announcements.

    Load more

    Appending results is an action, not a destination. Use a regular button outside a pagination navigation landmark, and supply application code to fetch and append results, manage busy and unavailable states, and announce updates. No pagination-specific class is needed.

    <button type="button">Load more results</button>
